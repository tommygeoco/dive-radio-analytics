# Sponsor page — build ledger (2026-10-11)

Owner request (Tommy, 2026-10-11): a view of the analytics, on the same data, that
reads as a landing page and dashboard to send to sponsors — positive comments,
moments where brands were discussed (linked or shown), retention and viewer data,
averages, the number and quality of the people who call in, and the packages as
priced in the owners' records. A design auditor and a sponsor-conversion critic
iterate until desktop and mobile pass 10/10.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A third static page, `sponsors.html`, reading `data.js` like the other two | One data set, no server, no runtime fetch (rule 10); the dashboard stays the owners' tool and keeps its views |
| D2 | Every sponsor number is projected in L2 (`sponsor-view.mjs` → `data.sponsor`) | The page formats, never derives (rule 4, validator 1j/1s) |
| D3 | Views on the page are the brief's show totals, field by field | One definition of "views so far" — absent unless every episode has its reading |
| D4 | "A typical show" is `baselines.windowFor` (the eight before the newest) + `trueMedian`, three or nothing; break-touched measures read only the newest side | The codebase's one meaning of typical (rule 16); it equals the show-health read's live typicals on the same day |
| D5 | Live viewers are never added across shows | One person watches many shows; a sum would overstate the audience |
| D6 | Subscribers and followers are summed as channel counts and labelled so ("one person may follow more than one channel") | A sum of counts, not a count of people |
| D7 | Call-ins come from the owners' published episode notes (`contributors-import.mjs`), names and words only | The notes are checked against the recording before posting; links stay private; E1–E3 have no list (absent, not zero) |
| D8 | Timestamps link only on a matched clock (`clock.mjs`, `clock-check.mjs`) | Measured 2026-10-11: Restream transcripts sit −5.4…+2.4 s from the uploads, spread 0.8 s — not "a few minutes". The same rule now drives the dashboard's chapters and the brief (definition-lock) |
| D9 | Brands are case-sensitive whole words in the transcripts; partner reads need a sponsor phrase within 15 s and skip "not sponsored by" | A misheard name is missed, never added; a partner is never credited with a moment the transcript does not give it |
| D10 | Quotes: named, positive, public comments (X, YouTube) only, shown as stored less addressees and a trailing link; one per person; by likes | Anonymous live chat and questions stay off a sales page; nothing is reworded |
| D11 | Packages are static copy from the owners' records and the signed IOs | Business terms, not measurements: Mid-roll $7,500/mo, Segment $12,500/mo, Build $17,500/mo; three months up front with 10% off; one partner per category; Build = one 10–15 min live build a month, named every episode, logo on intro/overlay/notes, usage rights; three build partners at a time |
| D12 | One booking address constant (`CONTACT`), default `hello@dive.club` | The owners confirm the inbox; the validator keeps it to one place |
| D13 | `noindex` | A kit sent by link, with prices; not a search landing page |
