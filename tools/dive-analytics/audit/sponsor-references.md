# Sponsor page reference board (2026-10-18)

Research for the 2026-10-18 rebuild (ledger D35–D36): eight real sponsor and advertise pages of creator-led
media, captured at 1440×900 and 390×844 and read for their order, first screen, proof, offer and path to
yes. The critics `sponsor-design-critic` and `sponsor-marketing-critic` judge the page beside them; the
screenshots lived in a session scratchpad, so a critic re-captures the folds it needs. Copy is
paraphrased, never reproduced.

## 1. The eight references

### 1. Creator Science: partner.creatorscience.com
**Verdict:** The best overall. A creator-led newsletter + YouTube + podcast sales page that presents deep data honestly.
- **IA:** hero + stat band → 35-logo wall → per-channel live-number cards → audience demographics → readers' own reach (362M followers) + creator quotes → host bio → 6 format cards with real examples → sponsor testimonials → 6-step process → form.
- **First screen.** Desktop: 80px two-line headline, a sub-line with scarcity ("a few partners a year"), a filled pill "Start a partnership" + text link "See who you'll reach ↓", and 4 numbers (38px, 2–3-word labels: followers, newsletter, podcast downloads, YouTube views) on a blue gradient. Phone: identical, with the numbers in a 2×2 grid inside 844px.
- **Proof:** logos right under the hero. Creator quotes with faces. Sponsor quotes with role + company (e.g. a brand-partnerships manager at Circle), the result phrase highlighted. Host photo, awards, "investor in" chips.
- **Data:** two metrics per channel card. Footnotes define them (downloads = median for episodes 1–6 months old) and name the sources (Kit, YouTube Analytics, Spotify). % tiles, then gender/age/country bars per channel.
- **Offer:** six format cards (reach chip, one-line spec, a real example: audio read, video, newsletter block, post, reel). No prices; budget asked in buckets (<$5K … >$20K).
- **CTA:** one action (the form) from the header, the hero and the process; email as fallback.
- **Objections:** the process covers fit check, "written in the host's voice", your approval before it airs, and a tracked link per placement.
- **Phone:** same order, cards stack; 20 screens long.

### 2. Acquired Partnership Kit: partner.acquired.fm (Notion)
**Verdict:** The model for multi-month packages. A plain document that wins on proof, a price/availability table and outcome stories.
- **IA:** intro + press quote → 4 big stats → growth chart → audience → famous-listener quotes → approach (hosts photo, how segments are made) → 3 sponsor examples with audio → listener quotes about sponsors → 12-logo grid → impact per sponsor → packages (season, presenting tier, back catalog) with availability tables → inquiries → speaking → event photos.
- **First screen.** Desktop: logo, 40px title, two paragraphs, press quote, four ~60px stats (#1 tech podcast, 1M+ downloads/episode, NPS 91, 4.9 from 7,000+ reviews); the chart begins. No CTA. Phone: the text, then two stats.
- **Proof:** quotes from named CEOs (no photos); impact with numbers (customers driven, top promo code, 73 named companies visiting); "declining 95% of inbound".
- **Data:** first-180-day downloads per episode, labelled by episode, showing growth over the years; role and geography bars.
- **Offer:** 6-month seasons (4 episodes, 4 partners) + a presenting tier. Tables of timeframe, package and price, with status in red/green ("Sold Out", "1 Remaining", "Available").
- **CTA:** email only, near the end; direct with the founder or CMO.
- **Objections:** a linked one-page agreement template; a unique segment per episode; no agencies.
- **Phone:** chart images become illegible; the price tables overflow sideways, so the Price and Availability columns are lost.

### 3. Forward Future (Matthew Berman): forwardfuture.com/partnerships
**Verdict:** The cleanest short page, from a creator who runs a live show: the essentials only.
- **IA:** hero → logo marquee → 4-number band + CTA → 3 format cards → 6 audience-role tiles → 18-logo grid → form with "what you'll get back" → numbers repeated.
- **First screen.** Desktop: eyebrow, 72px "Reach [audience]" headline, a mono sub-line naming the newsletter, videos and live show plus "a small number of sponsors", one filled orange CTA, and the marquee at the fold. The numbers (48px) open screen 2. Phone: the same; the numbers sit just below the fold.
- **Proof:** marquee + grid; no testimonials.
- **Data:** 4 numbers; role tiles without %.
- **Offer:** formats only; the form asks budget and goal.
- **CTA:** "Start a conversation" ×2 → inline form. Beside the form: you get the media kit, audience, formats and pricing, and campaign ideas.
- **Phone:** numbers 2×2 inside a card. The sticky header's button is the reader "Subscribe".

### 4. Changelog: changelog.com/sponsor (+ /sponsor/pricing)
**Verdict:** The textbook dev-podcast layout: the number is the headline, the ads are playable, and a CTA pair follows every section.
- **IA:** dark hero → table of 9 playable sponsor spots → 3-step process → role bar chart + listener-employer logos; CTA band after each.
- **First screen.** Desktop: "We reach 100,000 developers weekly", a sub-line with 3 more numbers, two filled green buttons (Get in touch / Pricing), a phone number. Phone: the same, buttons stacked full width.
- **Proof:** the ads themselves; logos of listeners' employers labelled by job title; no testimonials.
- **Data:** five role bars.
- **Offer:** a per-show rate card: price ($3,000/week flagship … $200/episode), minimum term, estimated reach range over the term, listens per episode at 30–45 days vs long tail, inclusions, past episodes.
- **CTA:** two actions (mailto, pricing) ×4.
- **Phone:** the table becomes cards and the chart becomes horizontal bars; the footer runs two screens.

### 5. Dwarkesh Podcast: dwarkesh.com/p/advertise (Substack)
**Verdict:** Weak craft, but a buyer-first order and a quarterly video-podcast package close to Dive Radio's.
- **IA:** contact line → "tl;dr" (3 data bullets) → 3 example mid-roll videos → survey charts → partnership structure → button.
- **First screen.** Desktop and phone: H1, form link + email, three linked bullets (1.8M average views/listens per episode, role mix, 18.2% control >$1M budgets), the first video.
- **Proof:** the reads themselves; no logos or quotes.
- **Data:** survey charts with date, n≈660 and a sampling-bias caveat.
- **Offer:** a quarter of mid-rolls on ~6 episodes + newsletter shout-out, category exclusivity, an in-person event; no price.
- **CTA:** text link at top; one yellow "Get in touch" at the bottom.
- **Phone:** chart images shrink to tiny labels.

### 6. Linus Media Group: linusmediagroup.com/partners
**Verdict:** The video-creator model: each format is shown by an actual sponsored video.
- **IA:** "PARTNERS" + host pitch video + 4 stats → five format blocks (most with an embedded sponsored video) → 2 case studies with outcome bullets → logos → long form.
- **First screen.** Desktop: 173px headline, the host's "let's work together" video, two paragraphs, four 61px stats (150+ annual partners, 3,200+ sponsorships, 10+ years, 60% return-sponsor rate). No CTA. Phone: headline, video, text; one stat visible.
- **Proof:** case studies (100M+ views; 425K views with high retention); 60% repeat rate.
- **Offer:** formats only; the form asks channel, timing (ASAP … 6–12 months) and budget.
- **CTA:** an outline "Let's connect" submit, plus email.
- **Phone:** stats become a vertical list *with two labels swapped*; the logo carousel is clipped.

### 7. The Rundown AI: therundown.ai/advertise-with-us
**Verdict:** The social-proof benchmark for AI and dev tools: a full-colour logo wall and named sponsor testimonials. The text is weak.
- **IA:** hero over a campaign video → three grey "why" paragraphs + ~30 logos (Notion, Zapier, Vanta, Microsoft…) → accordion per newsletter → 3-step process → 8 sponsor testimonials → reader poll wall → availability + CTA.
- **First screen.** Desktop: 60px three-line headline over a busy blurred video, a sub-line (2.5M+ readers, 10K+ new/day), white "Get in touch". Phone: the same, harder to read.
- **Proof:** each testimonial pairs the sponsor logo with a quote, a face and name + title; "repeat sponsors are 80%+ of the calendar".
- **Data and offer:** inside closed accordions: subscribers, open/click, growth per day, role %, named employers, ad slots (some "Coming soon"); no prices.
- **CTA:** Typeform ×2. Promises a reply within 24 hours; booked months ahead.
- **Objections:** their editors write the copy; a report arrives a week later with impressions, clicks and the companies engaged.

### 8. The AI Daily Brief: aidailybrief.ai/sponsors
**Verdict:** The best show-native idea: the sponsor page doubles as the listener offers page.
- **IA:** hero with two paths → "On the Show Now" (9 current sponsor offers for listeners) → 4 "why" tiles → 3 policy cards → brand list → dark final CTA.
- **First screen.** Desktop: 84px headline with a highlight block, outline "This week's offers ↓" + filled "Sponsor the show ↓", offer cards below; no numbers. Phone: the buttons stack.
- **Proof:** current sponsors visible as live offers; 20 brands as a text list.
- **Data:** tiles are words ("Millions", "C-suite", "Global") plus "6/wk".
- **Offer:** monthly blocks; longer commitments pick first; host-read mid-roll + show notes + this page; "no one-offs".
- **CTA:** one filled email button with a prefilled subject.
- **Phone:** the brand pitch starts ~60% down, after the listener cards.

### Also captured
- **Smashing Magazine** (`smashing/`): a design/dev audience and plain per-format prices (€1,000–5,000), but article-length, and its only CTAs are inline text links.
- **Bytes / Fireship** (`bytes/`): an email address plus ~40 reader tweets. No numbers, formats or sponsors; a contrast case.
- **Dive Club "Ridd's stack"** (`diveclub/`): the co-host's partners (Framer, Mobbin, Lovable, Paper, Jitter, Granola, Genway), each framed as "how I use it". No buyer information, but it shows the brands and endorsement voice Dive Radio already has.

## 2. Patterns the best pages share

1. **Headline names the buyer's audience in ≤7 words** ("Reach …" / "We reach N …"). *Creator Science, Forward Future, Changelog.*
2. **3–4 audience numbers at ≥38px desktop, each with a 2–4-word label, in one band within the first two screens.** *Creator Science, Acquired, LMG, Forward Future.*
3. **Numbers per channel and per unit, never one blended total.** *Creator Science per-channel cards; Changelog 30–45-day vs long-tail listens; Acquired first-180-day downloads.*
4. **Every number carries its definition and source within one line.** *Creator Science, Dwarkesh (date, n, bias caveat), Acquired.*
5. **Past-sponsor logos within the first 1.5 screens.** *Creator Science (35), Forward Future (marquee), Rundown (~30).*
6. **For every format, show the actual ad (playable or viewable), with the sponsor named.** *Changelog, Acquired, Creator Science, Dwarkesh, LMG.*
7. **3–6 format cards, each = name + one-line spec + reach chip + example link.** *Creator Science, Forward Future, LMG, AI Daily Brief.*
8. **The package is a term with episode count, exclusivity and extras.** *Acquired (6 months = 4 episodes, 4 partners), Dwarkesh (quarter ≈ 6 episodes, category exclusivity, event), AI Daily Brief (month-plus), Changelog (4-week minimum).*
9. **Price or availability is visible, or budget is asked in buckets.** *Acquired (price + Sold Out / 1 Remaining), Changelog rate card, Smashing; Creator Science, LMG.*
10. **Expected reach for the package is given as a range over the term.** *Changelog.*
11. **Scarcity is stated once, plainly.** *Creator Science, Forward Future, Acquired, Rundown, AI Daily Brief.*
12. **Sponsor testimonials carry name + title + company (+ face), kept apart from fan praise; the best highlight the result phrase.** *Creator Science, Rundown, Acquired.*
13. **Outcome stories: one named sponsor + one number each.** *Acquired, LMG, Rundown.*
14. **Audience composition as 5–6 plain role bars or % tiles, not a paragraph.** *Changelog, Creator Science, Acquired, Dwarkesh.*
15. **Host face and a short bio**, the trust anchor for host reads. *Creator Science, Acquired; LMG puts the host on camera in the hero.*
16. **One conversion action as a filled button, repeated 2–4×; the secondary is a text link.** *Changelog, Forward Future, Creator Science, Rundown.*
17. **The page ends in the conversion block: a 6–9-field form (budget, timing/channel, goal) or one prefilled email button, with an email fallback.** *Creator Science, Forward Future, LMG, AI Daily Brief.*
18. **Say what happens after contact** (reply time, what arrives). *Rundown, Forward Future.*
19. **3–6 numbered steps that settle control objections:** who writes, approval before airing, reporting. *Creator Science, Rundown, Changelog.*
20. **Sell value beyond the read:** an offers page, live events, cross-channel bundles. *AI Daily Brief, Acquired, Dwarkesh, Creator Science.*
21. **Freshness is claimed and shown** ("live numbers, not a stale media kit"). *Creator Science.*
22. **Phone keeps desktop order:** number bands go 2×2, logos 3-across, buttons full width. *Creator Science, Changelog, Forward Future.*
23. **No FAQ block on any of the eight.** Objections are answered in the process, package terms and agreement. Treat a FAQ as optional.

## 3. Anti-patterns the best pages avoid
- The pitch as grey paragraphs (Smashing; Rundown's "why").
- Primary action as a text link or outline button (Smashing, Dwarkesh top, LMG submit).
- Word tiles instead of numbers ("Millions", "Global"), or undefined metrics ("monthly footprint").
- One number disagreeing across surfaces (Smashing 514K vs 510K pageviews), or labels bound to the wrong number on phone (LMG).
- Stale or unfinished copy ("any Tuesday in 2025", "Coming soon" formats, typos).
- Headline over busy video (Rundown).
- Chart images and wide tables that break on phone (Acquired, Dwarkesh).
- Fan-love walls with no numbers or offer (Bytes); unreadable screenshot walls (Rundown).
- Formats hidden in collapsed accordions (Rundown, Smashing).
- Buyer content buried on phone (AI Daily Brief); clipped carousels (LMG).
- Header button serving readers rather than the buyer (Forward Future and Dwarkesh "Subscribe"; Rundown).

## 4. Typical measurements (the 8 references)
| Measure | Median | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Sections (hero + H2-level) | 7 | 4–12 |
| Desktop length (900 px screens) | ~6 | 3.6 – ~13.7 |
| Phone length (844 px screens) | ~9.7 | 4.6 – ~21 |
| Distinct conversion actions | 1 (+1 secondary) | 1–2, repeated 1–4× |
| Headline length | 5 words | 1–11 |
| Words on page | ~570 | 325–1,530 |
| Audience numbers in desktop first screen | 3–4 on 5 of 8 pages | 0–4 |
