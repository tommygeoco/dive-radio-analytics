## Step 1 — tokens and typeface to 3.1.1

- **Tokens.** Each page inlines the system's `tokens.css` verbatim (was a hand-trimmed copy of
  157 tokens; steward U13). The page's one product token stays after it: `:root { --gap: 16px; }`.
- **Typeface.** Inter, Latin subset, from `scripts/inline-fonts.mjs` (98 KB of CSS per page,
  replacing 71 KB of Geist + Geist Mono; with the verbatim token file each page grows 34 KB:
  `index.html` 382 → 416 KB, `agents.html` 85 → 119 KB). One face for words and figures. `body`
  drops Geist's `cv11` (in Inter it swaps the "a").
- **Figures.** `.n` / `[data-fold-number]` match the system's `.ss-num`: `--font-num`,
  `tabular-nums` + `"tnum"`, `white-space: nowrap`, `overflow-wrap: normal`; Geist Mono's −0.01em
  tracking is gone. Every rule that named `--font-mono` names `--font-num` and sets tabular figures
  (five rules had relied on Geist Mono's implicit tabular digits: `.bep`, `.epk`, `.panel .chip b`,
  `.tt .row .ep`, `.dtabs button .cnt`). The hero figure is tracked −0.02em, no longer −0.04em
  (principles §3: never tighter on a figure).
- **Inter's tabular forms are wider than digits.** `tnum` in Inter also widens the hyphen
  (0.43 → 0.65 em), parentheses, period, comma, colon and space. Tabular figures therefore ride on
  the figure runs only: the table's title and gap cells, the pane's `.pnum` / `.row .val`, the
  feedback pills and the Actions emphasis (`.insight .lead b`) no longer set `tabular-nums` on their
  words — their figures are already `.n` (`monoFigures()` wraps them). The Agents page's address is
  `.addr`, not `.n`. Measured: 0 word containers render with tabular forms (was "behind - the -
  scenes", "Call - Ins"); the list of proportional digit runs is unchanged (12 — titles, verbatim
  quotes, an anonymized name, as before).
- **Bar labels at phone width.** Inter's % sign is ≈ 0.84 em (Geist Mono's was one 0.6 em cell).
  On Today's "Share watched — by episode", twelve "11%" labels grew from 19.1 to 24.6 px against
  17–20 px columns: the gap between labels fell from 6.2–8.7 px to 0.8–3.3 px and the last label
  crossed the card's text edge by up to 3.7 px (360, 390 and 744 px viewports). Below 360 px of card
  the label is the bare figure under the measure's title (`@container (max-width: 360px)` hides
  `.bu`); each bar's accessible name keeps the unit. Now 11.5–14.0 px between labels, inside the
  edge; 430 px phones keep "11%" (6.7 px apart).
- **Charts.** Chart.js reads `--font-num` (Inter). Canvas text can't ask for tabular figures, so
  canvas ticks are proportional (the system's dataviz.md accepts it for static ticks). A
  `fillText` probe over all seven chart views, with and without a selection, at 1440 / 744 / 390 /
  360 px: 0 labels leave their canvas, before and after.
- **Agents page.** The prompt well and `<code>` names are words in Inter (`font-family: inherit`
  resets the UA monospace); the system has no monospace since 3.0.
