The inlined Inter is set to `font-display: block` (Smart Sharps 3.1.3's `inline-fonts.mjs` emits it, with `--script` for
the early load; `fonts.css`, a network font, keeps `swap`), and a
two-line script right after the page's `<style>` starts its load with `document.fonts.load("1em Inter")` before the first
layout. With `swap` the stand-in font set the 390 px hero one line longer and everything under it jumped (layout shift 0.14
on fast cold loads); `block` alone left 0.018 (the hidden text still laid out in the stand-in); both together measure 0 on
every network profile, with first paint unchanged.

**The dashboard pages take the same font loading (2026-10-18).** `index.html` and `agents.html` carry the 3.1.3 face
(`font-display: block`) and the early-load script after their first `<style>`; tokens and recipe runs stay 3.1.1 (the
font is outside both locks). Loaded, both pages are pixel-identical to before at 1440, 768 and 390. First-load layout
shift (no mobile emulation, cache off, local to 4G): `agents.html` 0 everywhere (it was up to 0.0065 at 1440).
`index.html` is unchanged at 0.17 (1440) and 0.51 (390), because its jump is not the font: the overview paints 32 px
tall and grows to 232 px when the data renders (`#overview` y 72→216, `.nextrow` 120→811 at 1440). That is queued
as its own fix.

The hero's source order is words, partners, picture (its caption first), numbers. The picture's inlined bytes are long, so on a
slow connection the page paints before the HTML after them has arrived; with the partners and the caption after the picture,
a slow load painted the picture and the numbers and then moved them (layout shift 0.016 at 1440 and 0.049 at 1024 on 4G).
The grid places each part from 64rem, the caption shows under the picture through `order`, and below 64rem the partners
take `order: 1` (words, picture, numbers, partners) and wait, `display: none`, until the numbers block's last part
(`#method`) has arrived. Loaded, the page is pixel-identical to the earlier order at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 360.

`[hidden] { display: none !important; }` follows the recipes, so a part the data cannot fill always steps
aside whatever its own `display` (the fixtures: no data, data late, partners ended, a short series, the
claim on its typical reading, one quote, two stats, no reviews, one host).
